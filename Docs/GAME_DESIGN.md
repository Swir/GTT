# Grand Theft Tractor — Game Design Foundation

## High concept

A comedic third-person open-world action sandbox set in a fictional countryside. The player can live legally, cause rural chaos, steal tractors and worn-out cars, take strange local jobs, fish, explore forests, attend village events and escape escalating police attention.

## Design pillars

### 1. Rural freedom
The countryside is not just scenery. Fields, dirt roads, forests, lakes, farms and villages are gameplay spaces with different vehicles, risks and activities.

### 2. Machines with personality
Vehicles should feel old, imperfect and memorable. Damage, smoke, poor brakes, improvised repairs and ridiculous upgrades create stories by themselves.

### 3. Consequences that stay funny
Crime should create readable consequences: witnesses, heat, police escalation and fines. Failure should produce funny situations rather than constant hard resets.

### 4. Dense comedy, not parody dependence
GTT should have its own fictional brands, places, characters and visual identity. The humour comes from systems and rural situations rather than copying another game's characters, logos, map or missions.

### 5. Small world, high interaction
A compact village with useful interiors and systemic NPCs is more valuable than a huge empty map.

## Core loop

1. Hear about a job, opportunity or local problem.
2. Travel using whatever transport is available.
3. Acquire tools/vehicle/items — legally or otherwise.
4. Complete the objective while systems react.
5. Escape consequences or pay for the mess.
6. Earn money/reputation/unlocks and discover the next opportunity.

## Wanted system

The prototype uses heat thresholds mapped to 0–5 levels. Crimes add heat, a delay begins after the last crime, then heat decays. The police director converts wanted level into a desired response strength.

Suggested escalation:

- **0:** normal world
- **1:** local patrol investigates
- **2:** active vehicle pursuit
- **3:** multiple units / roadblocks
- **4:** aggressive regional response
- **5:** maximum comedic countryside manhunt

## First mission — Borrowed Tractor

A relative's tractor has broken down before an urgent job. The player is told that a neighbour has left an old tractor nearby. The player reaches the farm, enters the tractor, gets spotted, gains wanted heat and must reach a barn/garage before the tractor condition or police pressure ends the run.

The mission teaches movement, interaction, vehicle entry, driving, damage and wanted mechanics in one short loop.

## World seed

The first greybox area should contain:

- player home/farm
- neighbour farm
- village shop
- workshop
- police station
- community hall
- lake/fishing spot
- forest edge
- fields and dirt shortcuts
- one paved road loop

## Content rule

Real vehicle brands, copyrighted characters, logos and copied maps are not required for the design. Use original fictional equivalents unless properly licensed.
