# GTT 0.1.32 — Farm Cargo Breakdown & Tow Recovery

## Added

- Added `UGTTFarmCargoBreakdownRecoverySubsystem`, an automatic runtime policy layer that connects active Farm Cargo exact-vehicle authority to the existing native breakdown and roadside-recovery systems.
- Added cargo-aware recovery states for degraded vehicles, tow recommendation, player-authorized tow, police impound, exact-vehicle absence and verified recovery.
- Added primary-save checkpoints immediately before cargo roadside tow / severe police impound movement and after successful exact-ID recovery verification.
- Added player-facing recovery guidance that makes it explicit that the contract clock continues and another vehicle cannot inherit the active load.
- Added 64-scenario manual/source playtest coverage plus a dedicated source-contract verifier and GitHub Actions gate.

## Changed

- Active Farm Cargo now explicitly treats native roadside recovery as a consequence inside the same route rather than an implicit escape from exact-vehicle authority.
- After a tow/impound transition the cargo policy re-verifies the same saved `PersistentVehicleId` before considering recovery successful; a missing/mismatched actor leaves handoff locked.
- Breakdown/tow integration deliberately reuses the existing `UGTTRoadsideRecoverySubsystem` for eligibility, cost, movement, damage preservation and police impound service. It does not duplicate billing or teleport logic.
- Recovery deliberately does not pause the Farm Cargo delivery timer and does not take ownership of cargo integrity, depot stock, payout, reputation or contract stages.

## Verification / release truth

- Roadmap completion remains 125/130 (96.2%). No Native Chaos, authored-trailer or Win64 acceptance checkbox is closed by this source milestone.
- Source-contract CI can verify authority wiring and anti-duplication invariants, but does not prove an Unreal Engine 5.8 Win64 compile/package or packaged-EXE runtime.
- Public demo release remains blocked until the exact candidate passes Win64 packaging/runtime, Native Chaos, trailer and rendered visual acceptance gates.
