# GTT 0.1.4 — Living CARGO Market & Dynamic Contract Chain

## Milestone

0.1.4 turns the existing Mulebox feed job into a reputation-gated rural logistics chain driven by the same village clock, fleet preparation, vehicle condition, police state, economy and primary save as the ROAD courier. The CARGO loop now changes with time of day and the player's delivery history instead of remaining a fixed one-stop payout.

### Reputation-gated CARGO chain

- **NEWCOMER / route T1** keeps the accessible `Feed Depot -> Hill Farm` contract.
- At **TRUSTED reputation (20+)**, CARGO route T2 extends the same loaded run through **Hill Farm relay -> North Wood Yard**. The timer, cargo integrity, loaded Mulebox physics and fleet-preparation penalty continue across the relay rather than resetting.
- At **RELIABLE reputation (50+)**, route T3 keeps the extended chain and raises the chain-completion bonus, creating a concrete progression reward without requiring a new disconnected job type.
- A dedicated final handoff terminal is spawned at the existing North Wood Yard by the farm-job director; Hill Farm becomes a relay only when the accepted route tier requires it.

### Living market and NPC-aligned schedule

- Feed cargo acceptance is staffed **07:00-17:30**, aligned with the existing villager work-schedule band. Vehicle PREP remains useful while closed; a ready contract cannot start until staff return.
- CARGO payout now locks a bounded market multiplier at acceptance. Morning rush, late feed demand, day-cycle scarcity, reputation and clean streak all affect the offered value; the multiplier is capped at **1.38x**.
- The contract-board terminal shows route tier, current market state, schedule, real CARGO completion/failure totals and the most recent delivery summary instead of adding separate debug telemetry.
- Police wanted blocks Hill Farm and North Wood Yard legal signatures while the same delivery clock continues. A police incident is remembered for reputation scoring even if wanted is later cleared.

### Persistent mixed logistics history

- Save schema remains **v8** because the new fields are additive and safely default on existing v8 profiles.
- v8 now also stores CARGO completed/failed counts, CARGO lifetime revenue and the six most recent ROAD/CARGO contract results (tag, payout and quality).
- ROAD courier success/failure now contributes to the same recent-history ring while preserving its existing reputation behavior.
- CARGO success/failure updates shared reputation, clean streak and lifetime logistics totals before `SaveProgress()`; failures persist their consequence immediately.
