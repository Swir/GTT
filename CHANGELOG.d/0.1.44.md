# GTT 0.1.44 — Packaged Workshop Hours & After-Hours Recovery Evidence

## Added

- Added `UGTTWorkshopHoursRuntimeEvidenceSubsystem`, an opt-in packaged route that begins after the existing 0.1.42 Farm Cargo workshop-recovery window and exercises the real 0.1.43 opening-hours economy.
- The route proves the live workshop boundary contract: 06:29 closed, 06:30 open, 19:59 open and 20:00 closed through the production `AGTTServiceTerminal` API.
- Added packaged proof that an ordinary damaged-but-mobile native road vehicle is rejected after closing before any economy debit or vehicle mutation.
- The same exact vehicle is then damaged into the production roadside-tow path; the evidence route requires request-time quote/target pinning, one exact tow charge and an authoritative `WORKSHOP HOLD`.
- Added packaged proof that a genuine after-hours hold uses the authoritative daytime repair quote plus exactly **35%**, rounded up by the shared policy, and that the displayed checkout quote equals the amount charged exactly once.
- Successful emergency service must repair/refuel the same `PersistentVehicleId`, clear the hard hold and leave the workshop closed; ordinary closed-hours service and emergency recovery therefore cannot silently collapse into the same path.
- Added `Scripts/evaluate_workshop_hours_runtime.ps1`, producing `WORKSHOP_HOURS_RUNTIME.json` (`gtt.workshop-hours-runtime.v1`) only from packaged PASS markers.
- Added `Scripts/promote_demo_gate_workshop_hours.ps1`; it requires the existing schema-13 Farm Cargo workshop-recovery proof and promotes a future exact candidate to demo technical gate schema 14 only after the new workshop-hours manifest passes.
