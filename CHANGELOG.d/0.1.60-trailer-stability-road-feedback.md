# 0.1.60 - Trailer Stability and Road Feedback

## Added

- Runtime trailer road-light feedback for tail, brake, reverse and hazard states.
- Bounded loaded-trailer stability assistance driven by speed, tow load and trailer health.
- Articulation-aware jackknife risk derived from tractor/trailer heading, road speed and live tow load, with smoothed warning behavior.
- Automated source and deterministic math verification for stability, jackknife and lighting behavior.

## Gameplay

- Loaded Heavy Haul driving is more stable at road speed while existing rough-driving and hitch-stress consequences remain active.
- A loaded trailer that folds into a risky road-speed articulation receives progressively stronger but still capped anti-sway damping, and the trailer hazards warn once sustained risk crosses the deterministic threshold.
- Braking, reversing and critical trailer condition are visible directly on the trailer.
- Damage reduces stability assistance, while a lost wheel disables anti-sway and jackknife assistance.
