# GTT 0.1.41 — Garage / Workshop Recovery Integration

## Gameplay

- Connect damage-preserving roadside tow consequences to the existing garage/workshop loop instead of letting a cheap garage recall bypass recovery.
- Vehicles whose authoritative fleet state is `TOW` or `IMMOBILE` now enter a hard **WORKSHOP HOLD** in numbered garage bays.
- The hold is evaluated from the same fleet/breakdown snapshot that already drives mission readiness, so no parallel damage ledger or fake service flag is introduced.
- `LIMP` and ordinary `SERVICE` states remain advisory: a mobile marginal vehicle can still be deliberately dispatched or driven to service.
- Garage bay interaction rejects a held vehicle before teleport/recall and before charging the garage dispatch fee.
- Garage bay labels and the garage office summary expose workshop holds and current damage-based repair estimates.
- The workshop recognizes the same shared hold policy. Native road repair/refuel service clears the underlying authoritative damage state, saves progress and therefore releases the garage hold naturally.
- Ordinary roadside tow still preserves damage and exact `PersistentVehicleId`, exits the vehicle at the workshop and does not auto-repair it. Police impound remains a separate safety-service consequence.
