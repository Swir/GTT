# GTT 0.1.54 — Civilian Incident Dispatch & Recovery Continuity

## Gameplay
- Disabled ambient traffic can now surface one bounded civilian roadside dispatch instead of remaining only a local crash reaction.
- The exact live incident receives a world-space `ROADSIDE SOS` marker and uses the existing player activity-message HUD channel for dispatch briefing, assist state, rebind, and closure.
- The existing 0.1.53 six-second roadside assistance remains the only repair/payout authority; the new dispatch subsystem never spends money, awards money, or mutates vehicle health itself.
- Active warden traffic control owns overlapping roadside scenes: a voluntary civilian helper timer is cancelled before completion while the warden stop is active nearby, without disturbing ranger search/fine/road-stop state.

## Continuity
- Added `GTT_CivilianIncident_01` schema-1 sidecar persistence for incident id, scene location, severity, and lifecycle history.
- Save/load deliberately does not persist an actor pointer, helper identity, repair mutation, payout, or the six-second helper timer.
- A restored incident rebinds only to a compatible disabled traffic actor near the saved location; otherwise it truthfully remains in reacquisition and expires after 180 seconds instead of inventing a vehicle.
- An interrupted in-progress helper job reloads as Active and must be restarted, preventing free progress or duplicate payout across reloads.

## Verification
- Added a 54-case playtest matrix and deterministic `Scripts/verify_0_1_54_civilian_incident_dispatch.py`.
- CI reruns the 0.1.53 roadside assistance verifier to protect repair-before-payout, no-theft, and single-reward behavior.
- This is source-contract evidence, not a packaged Win64 runtime proof. Unreal Engine 5.8 Win64 compile/cook/package, packaged EXE smoke, runtime evidence, and visual acceptance are still required for demo readiness.
