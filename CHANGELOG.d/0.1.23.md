# GTT 0.1.23 — Ranger roadside positioning and traffic reactions

## Gameplay

- Warden vehicle stops now use a single world-authoritative incident state instead of letting every ranger independently resolve the same vehicle contact.
- The primary ranger stages on the vehicle's shoulder side, slightly behind the stopped vehicle, rather than continuously pathing into the lane center.
- A second ranger no longer issues a duplicate citation. Reinforcement stages farther behind the same shoulder and supports the existing stop.
- Ambient village traffic now reacts to an active warden stop. Approaching traffic progressively slows inside a bounded 22 m safety approach and physically holds near the roadside contact.
- Search phase expands the traffic hold radius from 6.5 m to 8 m so a compliant driver and ranger are not immediately crowded by ambient cars.
- Traffic outside the road corridor, traffic moving away from the stop and traffic after the stop ends remain unaffected. No queue state is saved; cars recover through their normal route controller as soon as enforcement clears.
- Added a one-shot late compliance reminder before the seven-second window expires, while preserving the existing 2.5 km/h stop threshold and 2.25 s surrender/search hold.
- Fixed a consequence bug where a driver that had already fled a warden stop could be proximity-cited on the following ranger tick, effectively erasing the intended flee state. Evasion now remains a pursuit/police consequence until the wildlife incident resets.

## Integration

- Added `UGTTRangerRoadStopSubsystem` as the shared runtime authority for COMPLY / SEARCH / FLEE phases, road frame, shoulder side and traffic response.
- `AGTTRangerAIController` owns enforcement decisions; the subsystem coordinates spatial consequences but does not duplicate Wanted, citation or contraband state.
- `AGTTTrafficCarPawn` consumes the shared traffic response, promotes affected cars to critical performance updates, suppresses stuck-jump recovery while yielding and exposes `IsYieldingForRangerStop` / `IsHoldingForRangerStop` for authored presentation.
- Existing collision/incident response remains higher priority than road-stop yielding, so damaged or disabled traffic does not get artificially revived by enforcement logic.
