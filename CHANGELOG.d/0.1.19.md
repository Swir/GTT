# GTT 0.1.19 — Loaded authored-trailer runtime exercise

This milestone closes another evidence gap without pretending that a Win64 package or final trailer asset has already been verified.

## Deterministic loaded trailer route

- Added `UGTTTrailerEvidenceScenarioSubsystem`, a late packaged-runtime scenario that starts after the 0.1.17 drivetrain sequence.
- The scenario aligns the authored `tow_eye` with the Native Fieldmaster rear hitch, attaches the real `AGTTFarmTrailer`, loads cargo, waits for authored-rig takeover and dual-wheel contact, then tows the trailer through a controlled steering section.
- PASS requires at least 9 m of measured tow distance, at least 4 km/h, eight moving dual-contact samples, eight moving samples inside the 80 cm hitch envelope, cargo still loaded, the axle intact, and a controlled stop while still attached.
- The scenario emits `NATIVE_TRAILER_SCENARIO_SAMPLE` plus one `NATIVE_TRAILER_SCENARIO_COMPLETE` marker with distance, speed, hitch, articulation, cargo integrity and contact counts.

## Win64 acceptance hardening

- `evaluate_authored_trailer_runtime.ps1` now rejects a static parked-trailer pass. `NATIVE_TRAILER_RUNTIME.json` requires the deterministic loaded-tow scenario and records its motion metrics.
- The self-hosted UE 5.8 workflow keeps the packaged EXE alive for 172 seconds (195-second launch timeout) so the 126–172 second trailer scenario can actually complete.
- Packaged gameplay runtime duration validation is raised to the same 172-second evidence window.
- A dedicated 0.1.19 sanity workflow and the full Project sanity both verify the new contract.

## Acceptance honesty

- No final authored `.uasset`, UE 5.8 Win64 package, packaged EXE smoke, or rendered visual acceptance is claimed by this source milestone.
- The Roadmap remains 125/130 (96.2%) with all five runtime/hardware blockers open until real evidence exists.
