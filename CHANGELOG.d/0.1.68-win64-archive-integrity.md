## GTT 0.1.68 — sealed Win64 archive integrity

- Added `Scripts/verify_win64_candidate_archive.ps1`, a fail-closed post-compression round-trip verifier for the exact Win64 candidate archive.
- The verifier validates the ZIP SHA-256 sidecar, safe archive paths, exact full-tree coverage by `FINAL_SHA256SUMS.txt`, every extracted file hash, exact SHA/version/configuration identity, all attested evidence hashes/byte counts, and the single packaged `GTT.exe`.
