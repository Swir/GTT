# GTT 0.0.74 — Native Fleet Rollover Safety & Emergency Righting

## Added
- New `UGTTNativeRolloverSafetySubsystem` shared by the accepted Native Fieldmaster, Rattleback 82 and Mulebox 1200 takeover paths.
- Real Chaos wheel-contact sampling before anti-roll assistance is allowed to act.
- Speed/tilt-aware anti-roll torque that only engages with at least two valid wheel contacts, avoiding artificial airborne stabilization.
- Tire-integrity scaling so worn tires receive less corrective authority rather than gaining a hidden grip advantage.
- Driver-only emergency righting for a nearly stationary vehicle that remains beyond the rollover threshold long enough to be genuinely stuck.
- Recovery pulse cooldown to prevent repeated physics impulses from becoming an exploitable movement mechanic.
- Runtime evidence tokens `NATIVE_FLEET_ROLLOVER_EVIDENCE` and `NATIVE_FLEET_EMERGENCY_RIGHTING`.

## Gameplay impact
- Native tractors, cars and vans now resist progressive two-wheel lift through the same physics safety layer instead of each vehicle requiring a separate ad-hoc stabilizer.
- A player who genuinely rolls a Native vehicle can recover from a low-speed stranded state without an instant teleport or save reload, while high-speed and airborne states remain physics-driven.
- Tire wear now matters to rollover assistance strength, keeping the system connected to the existing workshop/tire economy.
