# 0.0.56 — Native Heavy-Haul Load & Sway Dynamics

## Added
- Canonical articulated-trailer tow-load factor driven by cargo mass, hitch stress, trailer integrity, axle loss and cargo integrity.
- Native Fieldmaster trailer discovery tied to the actually attached heavy-haul trailer.
- Trailer sway evidence from tractor/trailer yaw divergence and lateral relative velocity.
- Sustained-sway grace timer plus load-aware throttle cut, braking and severe-fishtail steering neutralization.
- Expanded `NATIVE_STABILITY_EVIDENCE` with tow-load, sway-risk and trailer attachment telemetry.
- `PLAYTEST_0.0.56.md` and `verify_native_heavy_haul_dynamics.py` Project sanity coverage.

## Changed
- Heavy Timber Haul now feeds real trailer/cargo condition into Native Fieldmaster stability instead of affecting only cargo/trailer integrity and payout.
- Loaded or damaged trailers trigger conservative intervention at a lower unsafe-speed threshold, while an unloaded/detached tractor retains the previous stability baseline.
- Tire tuning remains part of the shared handling loop and modestly offsets intervention without bypassing severe sway protection.

## Limitations / Next
- Source-level checks do not prove authored Chaos suspension/tire forces or packaged runtime behavior.
- Roadmap remains exactly 125/130 (96.2%); no Native Chaos or Win64 checkbox is closed without real UE 5.8 runtime evidence.
- Full Win64 compile/package, packaged EXE smoke and rendered visual acceptance remain required before any demo Release.
