## GTT 0.0.65 — Mulebox Cargo Dynamics & Fleet Role Integration

### Added
- Runtime `CargoLoadFactor` for Mulebox 1200, activated by the existing legal farm cargo contract rather than a disconnected vehicle demo state.
- Load-aware throttle moderation and progressive high-speed steering reduction routed into the existing Chaos fleet bridge input path.
- Mulebox-specific farm-role payout bonus so choosing the purpose-built van now has an economic as well as handling consequence.
- Explicit cargo-state cleanup on successful delivery, job failure and fresh contract start.
- `PLAYTEST_0.0.65.md` plus `verify_mulebox_cargo_dynamics.py` sanity coverage.

### Gameplay
- A loaded Mulebox is deliberately less responsive at speed and cannot use the same full-throttle/high-steer behavior as an empty van.
- The farm-job cargo state, vehicle handling, existing damage-sensitive cargo integrity and economy reward now form one connected loop.

### Limitations
- No claim is made that the Native Chaos Mulebox has passed a packaged UE 5.8 / Win64 runtime test; the source-level bridge and role dynamics are ready for that future acceptance pass.
- Roadmap remains 125/130 (96.2%) because none of the five runtime/asset-gated tasks has been honestly completed by this milestone.
