# GTT 0.1.2 — Rattleback Road Courier & Traffic-Risk Economy

## Milestone

0.1.2 gives the ROAD fleet role its first dedicated legal gameplay loop instead of leaving the Rattleback 82 as a garage/tuning-only vehicle. The new contract reuses the existing garage, Native Chaos road vehicle, wanted system, service economy and unified contract board.

### Village Parts Courier

- Adds a fifth unified contract-board card, **Village Parts Courier**, bound to the saved `ROAD` loadout and the Rattleback 82.
- Adds `AGTTRoadRunDirector` with a complete route: accept -> collect sealed parts at the village depot -> timed run to North Wood Yard -> paid handoff.
- The director accepts both the Native Chaos Rattleback and its protected legacy fallback, but it verifies the persistent vehicle ID so a tractor or cargo van cannot silently satisfy the ROAD contract.
- A compact world-space pickup marker and delivery marker replace debug walls of text and make the new route playable without adding copyrighted assets.

### Driving risk, traffic and police consequences

- Shipment integrity is now a gameplay resource during the delivery leg.
- Sustained overspeed above the safe cruise threshold damages the shipment instead of being a free speed advantage.
- Worn condition, tires and body state from the authoritative fleet snapshot add continuous shipment risk, tying garage preparation directly to the road mission.
- Native Rattleback impacts consume the real `GetNativeImpactCount()` signal and immediately reduce shipment integrity; the run reports the loss to the player.
- The North Wood Yard refuses a legal handoff while the player is wanted. The timer continues, so provoking police during a courier run has an actual economic/time consequence rather than being disconnected sandbox state.
- Payout combines the base contract value, shipment-damage penalty, fast-delivery bonus and clean-run bonus. Successful completion pays through the existing player economy and saves progress.

### Unified contract-board integration

- The contract subsystem now spawns one RoadRun director when the map does not already contain one and adds the ROAD card beside the existing four legal-work boards.
- ROAD preparation uses the same service/refuel/tires/dispatch transaction as the other role loadouts. Existing `RoadRun` garage thresholds now have a real consumer.
- Active RoadRun work participates in the global legal-contract overlap lock, so players cannot stack another farm/rural/heavy-haul contract on top of it.
- Fixes the preparation confirmation summary to display `MaximumNetReward` after prep cost rather than accidentally repeating the gross maximum reward.

## Verification
