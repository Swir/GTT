# GTT 0.1.25 — Physical Shoulder Pull-Over & Warden Patrol Scene

## Gameplay

- Road-stop compliance is now spatial: slowing down beside the ranger is no longer enough. The player must steer into the incident's fixed shoulder target before the search hold can begin.
- Each stop freezes a stable road frame when the order is issued. The lane-control anchor, shoulder target, ranger staging positions and patrol-unit parking transform no longer drift forward with a moving target.
- The COMPLY HUD reports distance to the shoulder marker until the player reaches the pull-over zone, then returns to the existing low-speed/hold guidance.
- Ranger reinforcement still shares the single incident authority and stages behind the primary contact instead of duplicating citations.

## Roadside scene

- Added `AGTTRangerPatrolVehicle`, an original lightweight roadside support actor assembled from engine primitive geometry rather than third-party or protected vehicle assets.
- The patrol unit is pre-spawned hidden/non-colliding by `AGTTRangerDirector`, deploys behind the shoulder target for COMPLY/SEARCH, flashes alternating beacons, and leaves the scene automatically when the stop ends.
- The patrol unit is deliberately not part of the player-drivable garage/save fleet; it is presentation/support for the existing warden authority, not a second vehicle ownership stack.
- Same-direction traffic continues to use the existing progressive slow/hold controller, now against the frozen lane anchor. Opposite-direction traffic and civilian recovery behavior remain owned by their established systems.

## Validation

- Added `Scripts/verify_ranger_roadside_scene.py` and a dedicated `ranger-roadside-scene-sanity.yml` workflow.
- The new gate chains the complete 0.1.24 enforcement-UX verifier before checking stable shoulder geometry, spatial compliance, patrol deployment, duplicate-state guards, documentation truth, README discoverability requirements and the locked Roadmap dashboard.
- `Docs/ROADMAP.md` remains 125/130 (96.2%); none of the five Win64/native-runtime blockers is closed by source-only work.
