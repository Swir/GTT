# GTT 0.1.49 — Workshop Service Lifecycle & Check-In

## Added
- Deferred workshop appointments now have a real timed lifecycle: **READY → IN_SERVICE → AWAITING_PAYMENT → completed checkout** instead of repairing the vehicle immediately when its slot becomes due.
- The exact booked vehicle must physically arrive at a real workshop terminal to check in. Check-in persists the exact `PersistentVehicleId`, service start and service completion time in the existing schema-v1 additive workshop sidecar.
- Service duration is deterministic and derived from the actual vehicle workload: condition deficit, tire wear, fuel deficit, body damage and detached panels. Normal queued work takes **30–90 in-world minutes**.
- Leaving the workshop service area before completion pauses service safely: the appointment returns to READY, keeps the same locked quote and exact vehicle identity, resets only the service timer and charges nothing.
- Completed service waits in `AWAITING_PAYMENT` when cash is insufficient. An underfunded checkout does not block later due appointments.
- The physical workshop terminal now detects the exact queued vehicle and defers to queue authority, so direct walk-up repair/refuel cannot bypass the locked quote, no-precharge rule or persisted service timer.

## Changed
- Request-time quote locking and no-precharge behavior remain authoritative. Booking and check-in do not debit cash or mutate the vehicle; the exact locked quote is charged only after the persisted service timer has completed.
- Repair/refuel still uses the existing `ApplyNativeWorkshopService()` production mutation and rollback path, followed by primary progress save.
- Workshop queue status and workshop-terminal interaction text now report the queue state, exact locked quote and service/appointment timing instead of exposing an alternate instant-service path.
- A genuine `TOW`/`IMMOBILE` hard WORKSHOP HOLD still takes priority over queue authority and retains the existing daytime/after-hours emergency recovery lane.
- Additive lifecycle fields preserve existing schema-v1 queue saves and the legacy first-entry mirror used by historical packaged-evidence consumers.
- Roadmap truth remains **125 / 130 (96.2%)**. This gameplay milestone does not close Native Chaos, authored trailer, Win64 package/runtime or visual-acceptance gates.
