# GTT 0.1.9 — Reserved Contract Queue & Dispatcher Favors

## Added
- A real stock-backed CARGO reservation queue at the Feed Dispatcher. Negotiating a load can now protect the actual T1/T2/T3 inventory immediately instead of merely remembering a tier label; protected units disappear from free Feed Depot stock the moment the dispatcher commits them.
- Relationship-based dispatcher favors with four bounded hold windows: `NEW DRIVER` gets 35 minutes, `KNOWN` 50 minutes, `TRUSTED` 80 minutes and `PREFERRED` 110 minutes. Holds are always clamped to the existing 17:30 depot closing time and never bypass the staffed-desk schedule.
- Queue capacity progression tied to the established relationship system: new/known drivers can protect one order while trusted/preferred drivers can hold two real orders. The oldest protected order becomes the next authoritative Farm CARGO contract.
- Reservation expiry consequences. A missed pickup returns the protected units to depot stock exactly once, but adds backlog pressure because scarce inventory was blocked for a driver who did not arrive.
- Persistent reservation state in the existing additive schema-v8 logistics snapshot: reservation day, ordered tiers, protected units and expiry hours survive save/reload without introducing a destructive migration.
- `Scripts/verify_reserved_contract_queue.py` and `Docs/PLAYTEST_0.1.9.md`, wired into Project sanity.

## Changed
- `ReserveCargoContract` now consumes the oldest matching protected load without charging depot stock a second time. Unreserved contracts retain the proven direct stock-debit path, so the existing FarmJobDirector, cargo-integrity loop, Mulebox load factors, buyer demand settlement and payout logic continue to use one authoritative market.
- `GetActiveCargoOrderTier()` now prioritizes the oldest valid stock-backed reservation, then falls back to the same-day negotiated tier and finally the living-market recommendation. The Contract Board/FarmJobDirector therefore consume the queue through their existing integration rather than through a parallel mission system.
- Feed Dispatcher interaction still preserves the established `E NEGOTIATE` contract and trust-filtered T1/T2/T3 cycling, but a successful negotiation now attempts to place the selected order into the player's earned queue and saves the stock commitment immediately.
- Feed Dispatcher world text remains compact while adding `Qn/capacity`; detailed interaction messages expose the hold favor and queue. Hill Receiver and Wood Foreman keep their existing `E STATUS` hooks and now include the same reservation summary in the detailed briefing.
- SaveVersion remains 8. The 0.1.9 queue fields are additive with safe empty defaults and are aligned/clamped on restore. Existing v8 profiles continue to load without a migration reset.
- Roadmap remains exactly **125/130 (96.2%)**. This gameplay/economy milestone does not close the dedicated Native Chaos setup, authored trailer asset or real Win64 compile/package/runtime gates.
