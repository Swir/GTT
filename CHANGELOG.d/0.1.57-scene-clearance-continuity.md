# GTT 0.1.57 — Scene Clearance & Lane Reopening Continuity

## Gameplay
- Successful county ROAD SERVICE recovery no longer removes the responder instantly. The responder now remains for a deterministic nine-second `ClearingScene` handoff so the roadside scene visibly transitions from recovery to reopening.
- During clearance the responder switches to `ROAD SERVICE - LANE REOPENING`, keeps only the rear cone pair visible, and the traffic-safety layer contracts from the full 1,800 cm corridor to a 980 cm reopening corridor.
- Reopening traffic pulses use reduced severity (`0.20` instead of `0.38`) while preserving the existing 320 cm recovery pocket and 4.5 second per-car cooldown.
- The scene-clearance phase owns no cash, payout, repair, Wanted, mission, ranger, dispatch-open/close or fine authority. The 0.1.53 player payout, 0.1.54 dispatch and 0.1.55 no-payout repair handoff remain authoritative.

## Continuity
- `GTT_CivilianResponder_01` advances to schema 2 and persists the exact incident id, responder phase, remaining lane-clearance timer and scene location.
- Schema-1 saves remain accepted for legacy `NONE` / `EN_ROUTE` / `ON_SCENE` states; only schema 2 may restore `ClearingScene`.
- A saved clearance can reconstruct the project-owned responder at the stored scene location even after the authoritative incident has already closed, then finish reopening and clear the sidecar without replaying repair or payout.
- New incidents cannot steal the old scene while `ClearingScene` is active; the old responder finishes its bounded reopening first, then the normal dispatcher may service later incidents.
