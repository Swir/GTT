# GTT 0.1.10 — Shared ROAD/CARGO Priority Dispatch & Rush Contracts

## Added
- A shared rural priority-dispatch model derived from the existing persistent logistics market instead of a parallel mission table. ROAD and CARGO each expose urgency levels `STANDARD`, `PRIORITY`, `RUSH` and `CRITICAL` from real stock, buyer demand, backlog pressure, commodity rotation and the active stock-backed CARGO tier.
- ROAD urgency is specifically tied to `FARM PARTS` days and North Wood Yard pressure. Priority thresholds are deterministic: wood demand plus twice backlog pressure enters PRIORITY at 5, RUSH at 9 and CRITICAL at 14.
- CARGO urgency reflects the strongest Hill Farm/Wood Yard buyer need, twice backlog pressure and active order tier. It remains meaningful even when free stock is zero if a real 0.1.9 reservation already protects deliverable units.
- A dispatch recommendation choosing `RATTLEBACK 82 / ROAD`, `MULEBOX 1200 / CARGO` or a neutral ready logistics vehicle from the same live market state. Equal urgency is resolved by actual buyer pressure rather than a random preference.
- Real ROAD rush economics: PRIORITY/RUSH/CRITICAL parts contracts multiply the existing road reward by `1.06x / 1.12x / 1.18x` while tightening the delivery window to `94% / 88% / 82%`. The multiplier is locked at contract acceptance and the time window at physical pickup, preserving the existing two-leg parcel, collision, police and fleet-readiness loops.
- Dispatcher-facing priority briefings. Feed Dispatcher, Hill Receiver and Wood Foreman expose the shared dispatch state through their existing interactions while keeping the compact `E NEGOTIATE` / `E STATUS` world prompts intact.
- `Scripts/verify_priority_dispatch_rush.py`, `Docs/PLAYTEST_0.1.10.md` and an independent `GTT 0.1.10 priority dispatch sanity` workflow.

## Changed
- Rattleback courier acceptance now combines the existing late-shift/reputation/supply multiplier with the new bounded ROAD urgency multiplier. High demand therefore produces a real risk/reward choice instead of a cosmetic urgency label.
- Rattleback pickup now locks the urgency-specific timer and reports the resulting delivery window. Parcel integrity, native impact damage, mechanical-risk degradation, wanted handoff blocking, fast/clean bonuses and reputation settlement remain authoritative.
- Rural dispatcher detail messages now report both urgency lanes, recommended vehicle, free stock, Hill/Wood demand and backlog. CARGO urgency is deliberately a routing/priority signal in 0.1.10; its existing stock/demand/route-tier economy remains unchanged and no hidden extra payout multiplier is stacked onto it.
- SaveVersion remains 8 because priority state is fully derived from already-persisted market, backlog, rotation and reservation state. Save/load therefore reconstructs the same dispatch priority without another migration surface.
- Roadmap remains exactly **125/130 (96.2%)**. This milestone does not claim any of the five remaining Native Chaos/authored-trailer/real-Win64 gates.
