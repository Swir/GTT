# GTT 0.0.98 — Native Road Fleet Tuning & Persistent Garage Service

## Added
- Direct Native Chaos service authority for Rattleback 82 and Mulebox 1200: tire repair, engine upgrades, tire upgrades and exact partial refuelling now mutate the active Native migration state and immediately synchronize its persistence mirror.
- Fuel-only workshop route for mechanically healthy Native road vehicles, priced by missing litres instead of charging a full damage-repair service fee.
- Garage fleet service planner exposing fuel litres/capacity, engine/tire tune levels, next recommended service action and applicable fuel/tire/tuning costs for each owned slot.
- `PLAYTEST_0.0.98.md` and `verify_native_road_fleet_tuning.py` covering Native authority, real drive effects, persistence, service economics and roadmap style-lock regression.

## Changed
- The tuning terminal now prioritizes an active Native Rattleback/Mulebox and can no longer accidentally upgrade the hidden legacy mirror that Native synchronization would overwrite moments later.
- Native road tuning remains mechanically meaningful: engine levels feed existing throttle power and fuel-efficiency calculations; tire levels feed steering grip and runtime wheel-risk assistance.
- Workshop, fuel-only service and tuning operations now call normal `SaveProgress()` after successful payment/application so paid garage progression is not lost on the next load.
- Native road workshop prompts distinguish healthy low-fuel vehicles from mechanically damaged vehicles and show the relevant price before purchase.
- Garage office summaries now combine condition/fuel/tire/body state with E/G tune levels and a compact `NEXT` service recommendation.
