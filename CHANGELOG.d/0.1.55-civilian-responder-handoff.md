# GTT 0.1.55 — Civilian Incident Responder Handoff & Rural Emergency Scene

## Gameplay
- Severe unresolved civilian dispatches now get an **18-second player-first grace** before county road service commits, preserving the existing paid player roadside-assistance opportunity.
- Added an original physical `AGTTRoadsideResponderVehicle` with runtime basic-shape body, cyan ROAD SERVICE presentation, alternating beacons and force/torque scene approach.
- Once the responder reaches the exact tracked incident, the traffic pawn owns a short seven-second responder recovery handoff; player roadside payout is locked out only while responder scene authority is active.
- Responder recovery is intentionally no-payout: it restores enough condition for the civilian to rejoin traffic under a temporary limp state, while the authoritative 0.1.54 dispatch observes that real vehicle recovery and closes normally.
- Warden traffic control has higher authority. An overlapping warden scene cancels/yields county response without mutating ranger search, fine or road-stop state.

## Persistence
- Added `GTT_CivilianResponder_01` schema-1 sidecar for exact dispatch id, responder phase, grace elapsed and on-scene hold remaining.
- The sidecar never persists actor pointers, player economy, Wanted state, repair mutation or ranger state.
- A restored EN_ROUTE/ON_SCENE response waits for the matching authoritative dispatch and exact tracked traffic vehicle before recreating physical responder state; stale/different incidents fail closed.

## Verification
- Added a 60-case playtest matrix and deterministic source-contract verifier.
- CI reruns the 0.1.54 civilian dispatch and 0.1.53 roadside-assistance contracts so repair-before-payout, single player reward, non-stealable traffic and warden priority remain protected.
- This is source-contract evidence only. A qualifying Unreal Engine 5.8 Win64 compile/cook/package, packaged EXE smoke, runtime evidence and visual acceptance are still required before demo release.
