# GTT 0.1.58 — Trailer Roadside Recovery & Suspension

## Added
- Player-facing timed field repair directly on the physical farm trailer through the existing `E` interaction path.
- Request-time locked trailer repair quote and deterministic repair duration with live remaining-time state for HUD/objective presentation.
- Safe cancellation when the player leaves the scene, the trailer starts moving, hitch tension becomes unsafe, a collision interrupts service or the player manually cancels.
- Exact one-time economy checkout on successful completion plus rollback/refund protection if the production repair cannot be applied.
- Completed-repair count owned by the trailer so repeat repairs preserve the established heavy-haul escalation model without a second charging authority.
- Heavy Haul integration that observes completed physical trailer repairs and applies the existing 22-second contract-time consequence exactly once per successful service.

## Changed
- Farm-trailer wheel constraints now use bounded vertical suspension travel with position/velocity drive instead of locking the Z axis; unloaded and timber-loaded states use distinct spring/damping tuning.
- Loading/unloading timber retunes intact wheel constraints for the new mass state while preserving free wheel spin and breakable axle thresholds.
- Heavy Haul delegates roadside service to the trailer's authoritative timed repair flow instead of immediately charging cash and mutating damage itself.
- Objective text now exposes field-repair quote or live service countdown from the same trailer state used by the interaction.
- Roadside repair continues to restore wheels/structure only; existing cargo damage is deliberately preserved and perfect workshop condition is not granted.

## Verification / release boundary
- Dedicated 40-case playtest contract and source verifier protect the interaction, transaction, cancellation, suspension and Heavy Haul integration rules.
- Roadmap remains exactly **125/130 (96.2%)**. No Native Chaos, authored trailer-asset or Win64 runtime checkbox is closed by this source milestone.
- Full Unreal Engine 5.8 Win64 compile/package/runtime smoke and visual acceptance remain required before a demo Release.
