# GTT 0.1.31 — Fleet Identity & Packaged Farm Cargo Recovery Evidence

## Gameplay / persistence hardening

- Added `UGTTVehicleIdentitySubsystem` for legacy fleet actors. Same-model vehicles may still begin with their class-level canonical ID, but later unowned duplicate instances are assigned deterministic actor-suffixed persistent IDs before they can enter ownership/save flows.
- Persisted/owned vehicle IDs are treated as immutable. The identity subsystem reserves owned IDs first and reports an already-persisted collision instead of silently renaming a vehicle behind an existing garage or cargo record.
- Added the guarded `AGTTVehicleBase::AssignPersistentVehicleIdForInstance` hook. It rejects empty IDs and refuses to mutate occupied/owned vehicles.
- This closes an important same-model ambiguity exposed by 0.1.30: two Mulebox actors previously inherited the same `Mulebox1200` ID, so an exact-ID cargo rebind could not distinguish them after actor recreation.

## Packaged Farm Cargo recovery exercise

- Added `UGTTFarmCargoRecoveryEvidenceSubsystem`, enabled only by the deterministic packaged evidence flags.
- The new route runs after the existing 0.1.29 Farm Cargo vertical-slice exercise and uses the real Start/Pickup/Hill Farm/North Wood Yard terminals, real primary `SaveProgress`/`LoadProgress`, real physical cargo authority, real logistics reputation and real economy.
- The loaded checkpoint now proves a stronger recovery path: save while `DeliverCargo`, destroy the original loaded Mulebox, recreate a new actor carrying the exact saved persistent ID, clear in-memory route state, reload the real primary save, then require exact actor rebind plus timer, integrity and stock restoration.
- After the loaded reload the scenario deliberately parks a different same-model van at Hill Farm while the true cargo vehicle is outside the handoff zone. The handoff must remain rejected after reload.
- A second checkpoint is taken after the Hill Farm relay. Reload must restore `DeliverFinalStop`, the same persistent vehicle, timer/integrity and unchanged stock before North Wood Yard can complete the route.
- Completion is reloaded once more to prove the route does not resurrect and payout/completion history/reputation do not duplicate.

## Evidence / CI

- Added `Scripts/evaluate_farm_cargo_recovery_runtime.ps1`, producing `FARM_CARGO_RECOVERY_RUNTIME.json` with schema `gtt.farm-cargo-recovery-runtime.v1`.
- The evaluator requires explicit PASS evidence for same-model identity uniqueness, loaded checkpoint save/load, recreated-actor rebind, wrong-vehicle rejection after reload, relay checkpoint save/load, timer/integrity/stock restoration, one authoritative final completion, completion reload idempotence, authority cleanup and final save.
- Extended packaged smoke launch with `-GTTFarmCargoRecoveryScenario` and extended the deterministic NullRHI evidence window to 228 seconds with a 250-second timeout.
- The Win64 evidence workflow now evaluates and uploads `FARM_CARGO_RECOVERY_RUNTIME.json`; missing/failed recovery evidence blocks `DEMO_TECHNICAL_GATE.json`.
- Demo technical evidence schema advances to 8 and now requires both the original `FARM_CARGO_RUNTIME.json` route and the new mid-route recovery manifest.
- Updated the 0.1.29 verifier so later evidence milestones may safely extend the runtime window without weakening the established 200-second floor.
- Added a dedicated 0.1.31 source sanity workflow, changelog and 80-scenario playtest matrix.
