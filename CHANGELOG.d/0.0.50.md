# GTT 0.0.50 — Heavy-Haul Roadside Recovery

## Added
- Paid roadside trailer repair integrated directly into the active Heavy Timber Haul contract and player economy.
- Trailer-side `PerformRoadsideRepair()` that restores broken wheel/axle constraints without resetting cargo, contract stage or the whole trailer actor.
- Escalating service price based on prior roadside repairs plus an extra charge for each lost wheel.
- Real gameplay time penalty for roadside work so emergency repair trades cash and delivery time for continued contract viability.

## Changed
- Broken trailer wheels are repositioned, re-enabled for physics/collision and re-constrained to the trailer body during recovery.
- Roadside repair restores only partial trailer integrity (capped below full workshop condition); crash and cargo damage remain meaningful.
- Heavy-haul objective text exposes when roadside recovery is relevant rather than forcing a silent contract restart after axle failure.
- Failed repair transactions refund the player instead of consuming cash without restoring the trailer.
