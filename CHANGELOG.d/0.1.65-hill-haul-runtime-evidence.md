## GTT 0.1.65 — Packaged Hill-Haul Runtime Evidence

- Extended Native Fieldmaster runtime telemetry with the production 0.1.62–0.1.64 hill-haul and trailer-brake state: terrain throttle authority, travel grade, hill-haul brake, hill hold, downhill tow brake, brake heat/authority/state, fade/cooling and bounded runaway mitigation.
- Added `evaluate_fieldmaster_hill_haul_runtime.ps1`, a fail-closed packaged Win64 evaluator that binds evidence to the exact candidate SHA and requires clean Native Chaos authority plus at least two loaded trailer samples, at least one real hill hold/downhill-assist sample and at least one real trailer-brake thermal behavior sample.
- Wired `FIELDMASTER_HILL_HAUL_RUNTIME.json` into the attested Win64 acceptance path. The exact candidate summary cannot be sealed until the hill-haul evaluator passes, and the final attestation hashes the manifest alongside the existing runtime, trailer, visual and EXE evidence.
- Consolidated the production `Win64 package evidence` workflow onto the same attested acceptance runner used locally, eliminating the stale duplicated package/runtime chain and preventing a second post-attestation ZIP build.
- Strengthened the reviewed Demo Release gate to re-verify the exact candidate attestation, hill-haul evidence and every digest in `FINAL_SHA256SUMS.txt` before human visual approval or publication can proceed.
- Corrected `Config/DefaultGame.ini` candidate identity to 0.1.65 so future packaging cannot silently label a new exact candidate as the stale 0.1.61 version.
- Added dedicated source/integration CI plus regression coverage for 0.1.62 hill-haul, 0.1.63 brake thermal control, 0.1.64 runaway safety, Native Chaos authority, progress presentation and the rolling exact-candidate/release attestation chain.
- This source/evidence milestone does not close any of the five roadmap gates. A real Unreal Engine 5.8 Win64 package must still produce the required runtime evidence, and the authored trailer/native movement plus human visual acceptance gates remain open.
