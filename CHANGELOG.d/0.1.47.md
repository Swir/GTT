# GTT 0.1.47 — Multi-Vehicle Workshop Appointments & Service Capacity

## Gameplay

- The after-hours workshop desk now supports **four** independent exact-vehicle appointments instead of one global reservation.
- Each vehicle keeps its own request-time locked repair quote, exact `PersistentVehicleId`, request timestamp and deterministic service slot.
- Appointments are spaced in **45-minute** service-capacity slots and roll to the next opening when the current day is full.
- Booking and exact-ID cancellation remain **no pre-charge** operations; cash is taken only when the exact vehicle is physically serviced.
- An underfunded ready vehicle no longer blocks later due appointments: its reservation stays unpaid while other affordable exact vehicles may proceed.
- Successful service removes only the serviced entry, performs the existing authoritative repair/refuel path, charges the exact locked quote once and saves primary progress.
- Failed workshop mutation refunds the exact debit and clears only the failed appointment.
- Hard `TOW`/`IMMOBILE` WORKSHOP HOLD remains on the separate emergency-recovery lane and is never converted into deferred capacity.

## Persistence and compatibility

- `GTT_WorkshopQueue_01` now stores an additive appointment array while retaining the original schema-v1 first-entry mirror.
- Existing 0.1.45 single-reservation saves can be restored into the appointment array without destructive migration.
- The first-entry mirror intentionally preserves the established 0.1.46 packaged single-appointment evidence contract.
- Restore de-duplicates persistent IDs, caps the book at four entries and keeps existing Farm Cargo / impound authority guards.

## Garage / UX

- Garage office presentation now reports appointment count/capacity and the next appointment instead of a single global `QUEUED` state.
- Repeated after-hours garage interactions can reserve additional eligible nearby vehicles until capacity is full.
- Player-facing copy explains exact-ID locking, locked quotes, no pre-charge and the separate hard-hold emergency lane.
