## GTT 0.1.53 — Dynamic Traffic Incidents & Roadside Assistance

- Wire ambient traffic condition loss into the existing collision-response state so meaningful real damage automatically becomes a traffic incident instead of depending on a disconnected helper call.
- Propagate non-recursive SLOW reactions to nearby traffic inside the incident-awareness radius while keeping disabled vehicles and ranger road-stop authority dominant.
- Add a player roadside-assistance interaction for disabled civilian traffic: 6-second proximity-gated work, cancellation when the helper leaves the scene, visible ASSIST progress, a 45% field repair and temporary limp recovery.
- Add a legal, severity-scaled $65–$110 economy reward with exactly one payout per disabled incident and no pre-charge/player cost.
- Keep ambient traffic non-stealable through the assistance interaction and force incident/assistance vehicles onto the critical simulation budget.
- Add a 48-case manual playtest matrix plus deterministic source-contract CI for damage wiring, nearby response, interaction distance/time gates, repair-before-payout ordering and single-payout authority.
