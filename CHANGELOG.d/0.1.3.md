# GTT 0.1.3 — Living Rural Logistics & Traffic Reputation

## Milestone

0.1.3 turns the 0.1.2 one-leg ROAD courier into a persistent rural logistics loop. The same Rattleback, garage preparation, Native road damage, police state, day/night clock, economy and primary save now drive a multi-stop job whose long-term payout changes with the player's delivery record.

### Multi-stop rural route

- **Village Parts Courier** now runs `Village Parts Depot -> Hill Farm relay -> North Wood Yard` instead of jumping directly from pickup to final handoff.
- The loaded timer and shipment-integrity model stay live across both legs; overspeed, weak condition/tires/body and Native impacts remain real economic risks.
- Hill Farm and North Wood Yard each refuse a legal signature while wanted. A police incident is remembered for the entire run even after wanted is cleared.
- The HUD objective and compact world markers switch through pickup, relay and final handoff without adding a new debug-text wall.

### Persistent logistics reputation

- Adds `UGTTLogisticsReputationSubsystem` with 0–100 reputation, clean-delivery streak, completed/failed run counts and lifetime courier revenue.
- Clean, impact-free, police-free deliveries build reputation fastest; damaged runs grow it slowly, police incidents reduce the gain, and failed deliveries reduce reputation and reset the streak.
- Reputation and clean streak increase future courier payouts, capped to keep the economy bounded.
- Primary SaveGame advances to **schema v8** and persists the complete logistics record while retaining v5 structural damage, v6 active dispatch and v7 role-loadout migration gates.

### Day/night logistics economy

- The Parts Depot accepts ROAD courier work from **06:00 to 21:30** using the existing village clock.
- Starting between **18:30 and 21:30** locks a **+10% late-shift multiplier** for the run, so crossing 21:30 while already loaded does not silently change the advertised payout.
- The unified contract board shows the live schedule, reputation tier/score and current payout bonus. A vehicle can still be serviced/staged while the depot is closed, but an already-ready contract cannot be accepted until opening.
- ROAD base/max values are now $310/$460 before reputation and shift multipliers, reflecting the added Hill Farm leg.
