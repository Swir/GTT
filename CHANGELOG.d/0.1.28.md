# GTT 0.1.28 — Farm Cargo Physical Vehicle Authority & Progress Evidence

## Gameplay

- Added `UGTTFarmCargoAuthoritySubsystem` so the physical cargo vehicle loaded at Feed Depot becomes the authoritative handoff vehicle for that contract.
- Hill Farm and North Wood Yard now reject a different vehicle even when it is parked closer to the terminal.
- Handoffs validate the original cargo vehicle itself: it must be inside the 750 cm buyer zone and moving at no more than 3.0 km/h.
- Tier-2+ relay jobs keep the same physical vehicle binding from Hill Farm through North Wood Yard; direct and final completion clear it, and failed/idle contracts are cleaned up by the subsystem.
- The subsystem deliberately owns no cash, stock, reputation, Wanted heat or save state. Existing `AGTTFarmJobDirector`, logistics, economy and game-mode persistence remain authoritative.
