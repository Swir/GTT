# GTT 0.1.36 — Roadside Dispatch Contract

## Added
- Player-authorized voluntary roadside dispatch now pins the exact `PersistentVehicleId`, locks the accepted tow/patch quote at request time, and exposes pending mode/quote/target/countdown through Blueprint-friendly query APIs.
- Same-key cancellation: `T` / D-pad Up cancels an inbound voluntary tow; `Y` / D-pad Left cancels an inbound emergency patch before arrival, with no charge taken.
- Cross-service conflict protection prevents a patch from silently becoming a tow (or vice versa); the current service must be cancelled explicitly first.
- Dedicated `Scripts/verify_roadside_dispatch_contract.py`, CI workflow and 68-scenario `Docs/PLAYTEST_0.1.36.md`.

## Changed
- Voluntary tow completion consumes the request-time locked quote rather than re-calculating price after damage/fuel state may have changed during dispatch.
- Voluntary patch/tow reject a changed target identity before charge or vehicle mutation/movement. A request that becomes ineligible, is interrupted by Wanted heat, or is unaffordable at arrival ends safely without negative balance or unintended movement.
- Police impound remains a separate automatic, non-cancellable consequence for genuinely stranded vehicles and keeps its own impound cost/safety-service behavior.
- Recovery prompts now explain locked quotes, arrival charging and repeat-key cancellation.
