# GTT 0.1.12 — Priority Route Planner & Consequence Ledger

## Added
- Shared ROAD/CARGO route planner that scores the live Rattleback and Mulebox choices from the existing urgency, fleet readiness, preparation cost, depot schedule, stock-backed reservations, destination demand and persistent failure/backlog history.
- A consequence ledger reconstructed from schema-v8 delivery counters and backlog. Failed ROAD/CARGO work creates bounded debt, successful work gradually earns that debt down, and the current backlog remains visible as separate pressure instead of disappearing behind one recommendation label.
- Cross-lane CARGO hold cap: when both lanes are urgent and the ROAD option is materially stronger, an urgent CARGO reservation remains playable but releases scarce Feed Depot stock earlier if the driver does not collect it. Score gaps of 15/30/50 progressively cap the emergency hold to 75%/65%/55% of its existing priority SLA, never below 10 world-clock minutes.
- Planner output is injected into the existing Contract Desk summary, so Feed Dispatcher, Hill Receiver, Wood Foreman and Contract Board surfaces that already consume the shared desk now expose one authoritative route decision instead of a separate debug panel.
- `Scripts/verify_priority_route_planner.py`, `Docs/PLAYTEST_0.1.12.md`, and dedicated `GTT 0.1.12 priority route planner sanity` workflow.

## Changed
- Existing relationship favors (35/50/80/110 minutes), 0.1.11 emergency SLA cap (40/25/15 plus earned chain grace), stock reservation, expiry stock return, backlog increment and no-double-debit pickup remain authoritative. The new conflict cap is a third minimum only when the planner proves a real competing ROAD emergency.
- Reserving CARGO feeds back into the next planner evaluation through the already-authoritative reservation count and protected stock. Letting the hold expire returns stock once, increases backlog once, and therefore changes later route scores without adding parallel mission state.
- Fleet readiness matters to dispatch advice: READY/ADVISORY/SERVICE REQUIRED/UNAVAILABLE, actual prep estimate, open/closed schedule and real buyer demand all contribute. The planner can recommend ROAD, CARGO, a close `SPLIT / CHOOSE COMMITMENT`, or waiting/service if both assigned vehicles are unavailable.
- SaveVersion remains 8. The planner and ledger are reconstructed entirely from state that already persists; no duplicate recommendation, debt or route-choice field can become stale in a save.
- Roadmap remains exactly **125/130 (96.2%)**. None of the five remaining Native Chaos/authored-trailer/real-Win64 gates is claimed by this milestone.

## Verification boundary
- Source sanity verifies planner scoring inputs, bounded failure debt, cross-lane hold thresholds, relationship-SLA composition, Contract Desk integration, schema stability and SWIR Roadmap Style Lock.
- Source verification does not prove Unreal Header Tool/C++ compile, real driving feel, packaged Win64 behavior or rendered presentation. These scenarios still require a real UE 5.8 Windows build.
- **No demo Release is authorized** by 0.1.12 alone. A verified Win64 compile/package, packaged-EXE runtime smoke, rendered visual acceptance, green relevant Actions and no demo-critical blocker remain mandatory.
