# GTT 0.1.6 — Dynamic Rural Orders & Supply-Chain Consequences

## Added
- Dynamic CARGO order selection that separates the player's reputation **capability tier** from the order currently offered by the living market. Strong Hill Farm demand can produce a direct T1 order even for experienced drivers; Wood Yard pressure can produce T2 relay or T3 bulk work.
- Persistent `CargoBacklogPressure` in the additive schema-v8 logistics snapshot. Failed/lost loads raise pressure, severe failures raise it faster, and successful direct/extended deliveries work the pressure back down.
- Order-facing APIs and Contract Board presentation for active order tier, unit count, route, priority and backlog state.
- T3 four-unit bulk CARGO with a real 1.20 Mulebox cargo-load factor plus extra route time, integrating the larger contract with the existing Native/legacy cargo handling rather than only increasing text/reward values.
- Shared ROAD/CARGO supply signal: when the rotating commodity is FARM PARTS, unresolved Wood Yard/backlog pressure contributes up to +10% to the existing Rattleback courier reward multiplier and is surfaced on the ROAD Contract Board.
- `Scripts/verify_dynamic_rural_orders.py` and `Docs/PLAYTEST_0.1.6.md`, wired into Project sanity.

## Changed
- Daily CARGO demand is no longer reset to unrelated deterministic values at midnight. Unserved demand carries forward, fresh daily orders are added within hard caps, and backlog pressure decays gradually after affecting the new work day.
- CARGO market reward pressure remains bounded: the previous base market calculation is still capped at 1.38x; saved backlog adds at most 0.10x and the final multiplier is capped at 1.48x.
- T1 still reserves 2 units and T2 reserves 3; T3 bulk work now reserves 4. Failed cargo remains lost from depot inventory while destination demand remains open.
- The unified board now explains `CAP Tn / ORDER Tn`, current order priority and unit count instead of implying that reputation always forces the same route.
- SaveVersion remains 8 because backlog pressure is an additive field with a safe zero default; existing v8 profiles keep their logistics/market/history state.
- Roadmap remains exactly **125/130 (96.2%)**. No authored-asset, Native Chaos runtime, Win64 package, runtime-smoke or visual-acceptance checkbox is claimed by this source gameplay milestone.
