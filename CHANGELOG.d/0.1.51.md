# GTT 0.1.51 — Workshop Priority, Pickup & Fleet Return

## Added
- Persisted STANDARD / URGENT workshop priority on exact-ID appointments. URGENT is a deliberate pre-check-in upgrade with a deterministic +20% locked checkout quote, earlier waiting-service priority and x0.80 service duration; promotion never pre-charges.
- Separate physical workshop priority desk beside the job board with a six-second two-interaction exact-ID confirmation guard, keeping quote-changing priority control separate from cancellation and pickup.
- Persisted `READY_FOR_PICKUP` state after successful timed checkout, including exact paid amount/date/time and vehicle identity.
- Exact-ID pickup release from the workshop job board. Pickup itself performs no cash or repair mutation and removes the completed appointment only after the paid repaired vehicle is physically present at the workshop.
- Garage-slot `PICKUP HOLD` so a completed paid vehicle cannot be teleported/dispatched around the pickup handoff; ordinary fleet dispatch resumes after release.
- Dedicated 80-case playtest and source-contract verifier covering priority, persistence, payment ordering, pickup, garage fleet return and Farm Cargo authority.

## Changed
- The authoritative queue now separates **service completion/payment** from **fleet release** instead of deleting a successful appointment immediately after repair.
- Queue SaveGame schema remains additive v1: old saves load as STANDARD waiting work with no fabricated pickup claim.
- The 0.1.49 workload-derived 30–90 minute STANDARD service remains the base; URGENT applies the documented x0.80 multiplier only after explicit promotion.
- Historical packaged workshop evidence remains a release gate and must continue to use the production service path; an evidence-only auto-pickup compatibility path preserves earlier sidecar-cleared expectations without changing normal gameplay.
- Roadmap progress remains **125 / 130 (96.2%)**. No Native Chaos, authored-trailer, Unreal Engine 5.8 Win64 package/runtime or visual acceptance checkbox is closed by source work.

## Verification / limitations
- Source CI verifies exact-ID promotion, +20% quote math, no-precharge promotion, persisted priority/pickup fields, debit-before-repair-before-pickup ordering, board authority isolation, pickup hold before garage recall, older workshop lifecycle/capacity contracts and deterministic SWIR SVG presentation.
- Unreal Engine 5.8 Win64 compile/package/runtime smoke and rendered visual acceptance are still required before any public demo Release.
