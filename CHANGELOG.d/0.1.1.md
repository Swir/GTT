# GTT 0.1.1 — Contract Board & Fleet Preparation Economy

## Milestone

0.1.1 turns the 0.1.0 mission-aware garage loadouts into a player-facing planning loop instead of leaving readiness information scattered across individual job terminals.

### Unified legal contract board

- Adds four live contract-board cards beside the farm garage for Feed Cargo Run, Heavy Timber Haul, Timber Delivery and Field Mowing.
- Every card reads the authoritative `UGTTGarageFleetSubsystem` assessment for its required `TRACTOR` or `CARGO` loadout.
- Boards show base/max payout, assigned vehicle, condition/fuel/tires/body readiness, preparation quote and best-case net reward before the player commits.
- A ready and staged loadout starts the existing real director (`AGTTFarmJobDirector`, `AGTTHeavyHaulDirector` or `AGTTRuralWorkDirector`); no duplicate mission state was introduced.
- The board prevents overlapping legal contracts and keeps police/game-warden locks intact.

### One-action fleet preparation economy

- `PREP` uses the assigned saved role loadout and charges a real preparation bill rather than faking a ready state.
- The package can combine workshop repair, tire service, refuel and $15 garage dispatch/staging depending on the actual vehicle snapshot.
- Native Rattleback/Mulebox service remains authoritative through `ApplyNativeWorkshopService`, `RepairNativeTires`, `RefuelNativeVehicle` and `FlushNativePersistenceMirror`.
- Native Fieldmaster preparation writes the legacy persistence mirror and re-imports it into the Chaos pawn before staging.
- Preparation sets ACTIVE + role loadout only after the transaction, saves progress, preserves wanted/warden restrictions and refuses occupied vehicles.

### Consequences for bypassing preparation

- Existing direct Farm Cargo and Timber boards stay usable for sandbox freedom, but starting with a CAUTION loadout now applies a 10% payout multiplier and SERVICE REQUIRED applies 25%.
- The objective/result text exposes the preparation penalty so the loss is not hidden.
- Heavy Haul and Field Mowing keep their existing hard safety block for critically unready tractor loadouts.
- Vehicle damage and cargo-integrity loss still apply independently, so abusing a worn vehicle can cost both the readiness penalty and physical cargo loss.

### Native Fieldmaster condition-unit hardening

- Audited the native tractor migration path after the contract-board integration exposed an old unit mismatch: legacy/save/garage health is a normalized `0.0..1.0` ratio, while older native collision and heavy-haul code still treated the same field as `0..100`.
- Makes the Fieldmaster migration snapshot explicitly normalized, including editor clamp metadata and a healthy default of `1.0`.
- Native collision damage now subtracts a normalized `0.13 * severity` health ratio and logs player-facing percentages only at the presentation boundary.
- Native Heavy Haul eligibility and reward condition factors now consume the same normalized health ratio as the legacy Fieldmaster, garage readiness, persistence mirror and contract board.
- This prevents a freshly imported healthy Fieldmaster (`1.0`) from being misread as `1%`, rejected by the 40% Heavy Haul gate, or effectively destroyed by one normal native impact.
